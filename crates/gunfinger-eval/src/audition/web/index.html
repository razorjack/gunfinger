    Keys

    Mouse: click or drag on a waveform to seek; click a marker to jump to it; click a question on the left to open it. Lanes play on one timeline: switching lanes keeps the musical moment. Speed matching plays each lane at its placement's speed (pitch and tempo together, like a turntable); without it a lane plays at its own speed and the playhead follows that lane. O places the aligned lanes by every alignment (the default: where the main alignment ends, the strongest other one takes over), by the main alignment throughout (one offset and speed, so two rips of one recording play the same moment end to end), or from their files' starts at the main alignment's speed; markers and shading still show the alignments. Each question opens placed by every alignment.
