seed is rendered. It fixes what steps 2-5 of `docs/brief-35.md` measure,

## Step 2: records that share material

Measured on the shared and beatmatched test mixes: method 2's count
(stretches at 10 hits, blocks of 30 s or more, overlapping ones merged;
session 34's fixed reading) and method 1 (registered interval: the
estimate, range A, range B) against the truth by k: exact, within one,
off by two or more, low and high. Merges (`shared_merges.py`): two
consecutive held-out plays inside one listed gap are a couple; a couple
is merged when one stretch overlaps the own part of each (the play
without its crossfades) by at least 15 s. Reported for the panel's pairs
and for other couples (the control's are all others), with truth minus
the count in gaps with and without a merged couple.

- **S1**: pooled over k = 2-4 (the runs that hold pairs), method 2's
  exact share in the shared mixes is at most 10 points below the
  control's, and its within-one share at most 5 points below.
- **S2**: at most 20% of the panel's pairs are merged.

Method 2 holds up against shared material when S1 and S2 hold. If not,
the experiment says how often and by how much, and step 4's combined
estimate is judged on the shared mixes as on the others.

## Step 3: the neighbour discount

**Rule.** Each listed gap is searched (the product's search and ladder)
against an index of its neighbours' records alone: the files of the
confident plays whose segments end where the gap starts or start where
it ends (`self-match --neighbours`). A stretch is discounted when
detections of a neighbour with at least NEIGHBOUR_HITS hits cover at
least NEIGHBOUR_SHARE of its span. Fixed values: NEIGHBOUR_HITS = 10,
NEIGHBOUR_SHARE = 0.5 (`gap_tests.py`): 10 hits is method 2's own
threshold per cell and lag. On the development data seen before this
commit (the long mixes, and 36 of the 45 NAS listings), no neighbour
detection of 10 hits or more lies inside a long-mix gap; at NAS scale
the only ones are Star Trails' tail (258-274 hits), which removes its
stretch from the gap after Star Trails in all 4 listings where that
gap holds nothing missed (the plain scan and 3 leave-outs)
and, in leave-outs whose gap also holds missed tracks, lowers the count
in 4 gaps, 3 of them away from the truth (the missed records there
made no stretch of their own).

- **D1** (development, where the rule was chosen; it shows the rule can
  work, it does not test it): of the 12 NAS gaps with no missed track,
  at least 10 count 0 after the discount. The 262-track gaps with no
  missed track are reported beside it.
- **D2** (test: shared and beatmatched mixes): the discount makes the
  count lower than the truth where it was not before in at most 2% of
  gaps holding 1-4 missed tracks, and lowers the exact share by at most
  2 points at each k.

The discount passes when D1 and D2 hold; otherwise step 4 uses the
count without it.

## Step 4: the combined estimate

**Rule.** The count (discounted if step 3 passes) limited to method 1's
range B (the quartiles of the clean intervals): below the range it is raised to the range's low end,
above it lowered to its high end. A count of 0 stays 0 (method 1 cannot
say 0). A gap without an estimate (fewer than 3 clean intervals) keeps
the count. Range B was chosen on the long development mixes, where it gives
exact 78, 69, 60 and 48 of 80 gaps at k = 1-4 and none off by two,
against range A's 76, 64, 59 and 48 with 3 off by two; most NAS
development gaps have no registered estimate (experiment 0160).

Scored on the test mixes (shared and beatmatched pooled, and rides and
long beside) and on the development gaps (NAS and 262-track leave-outs
and plain scans, long mixes), against method 1 alone and method 2
alone: exact, within one, off by two or more, by k; gaps with no
missed track, counted 0 or not.

- **K1**: the combined estimate is preferred to method 2 alone when,
  pooled over k = 1-4 on the shared and beatmatched test mixes, its
  exact share is at least as high and its share off by two or more no
  higher; to method 1 likewise.
- **Printing** (as session 34 registered for method 1): a count ("about
  n") when exact for at least 90% of gaps at each k = 1-4; a range of n-1
  to n+1 when that range holds the truth for at least 90% at each k and
  the estimate is off by two or more for at most 5% pooled.

## Step 5: pitch rides

Measured on the rides and long test mixes: method 2's count at speed
1.0, by k (exact, within one), and the indexed plays found confident.
Beside it, the same count with repeats at other speed ratios
(`self-match --ratios`): the neighbouring rungs (0.996, 1.004) and every
rung to ±3.2% (0.968 to 1.032 in steps of 0.004), with each run's time
against the run at 1.0 alone on the same gaps.

- **R1**: the count drops with rides when its exact share pooled over
  k = 1-4 on the rides mixes is more than 5 points below the long
  control's. If it drops, the ratio sets are compared (exact share and
  time); neither joins the combined estimate in this session.
