## Session 70

### What changed

The product did not change: the `gunfinger` binary is byte-identical to
`s69-final`'s. The harness's residual pilot (`mixes --kind drops
--residual`, session 65) gained, opt-in and tested:

- `--residual-timing` and `--residual-fix` take lists, so one run
  searches each case's plain and denser excerpts once and the masked
  residual (M, and MH without the partners' recordings) for every
  timing and fix. The report's `residual` nests the masked arms per
  timing (`maskings`) and fix (`fits`); its name adds `-fix-<fixes>`
  unless the only fix is `off`, so session 65's names hold.
- A measure of how far A's reference is off in time in the excerpt
  (`residual::lags`): per block of 8.2 s, the lag within 400 ms at which
  the reference's band levels (cube root of third-octave power) best
  correlate with the mixture's; a Theil-Sen line through those; each
  block refined within 3 frames of the line in 2 ms steps. The fix
  *Align* shifts the reference by that map before the gains are
  fitted; *Align, fine* also fits gains per 0.5 s instead of 1 s.
- Per case: the rip's line with the file the time map is on (hits,
  windows, speed), the seek offsets of the reference and played files
  (a render by seek against the decoded samples, 0127), and the gain
  fit's spread per band.
- Per masked detection at possible or above, the evidence the plain
  excerpt holds of it: the hits of the strongest agreeing detection
  when the excerpt over its span and 5 s either way is searched against
  its file alone on the rungs within 0.6% of its speed.
- Detected timing now takes its reference by 0257's rule (another rip
  where the store holds one, placed through the detected file's line);
  `ReferenceKind::Detected` is gone.

`scripts/analysis/residual_residue.py` summarises the new reports
(`--diagnose --probe` for step 1's measures). Both regressions stay
identical.

In short: another rip leaves A in the residual when it is another
transfer, 0.1-0.2% off in speed, which its stored peaks' line places
off in time in the excerpt (a median 12-28 ms); aligning the reference on the
mixture (*Align*) removes most of that residue. With *Align*, A's time
map from the whole-mix detection does as well as the generator's. On
fresh test seeds at NAS scale the residual then confirms 61 of 64 quiet
and brief partners (plain 41), 46 confidently (plain 15), with no wrong
confident answer. Needing evidence in the original mixture (step 3's
rule) removed no wrong answer and dropped 10 of those partners.

## Session 72

### What changed

`gunfinger identify --under` (ADR 0025, status Proposed), off by
default: a quieter track under a confident one, proposal 4 of
`docs/research/2026-10-29-matching-and-archive-proposals.md` built into
the product after the user's answers of 2026-11-02. Without the option
nothing changes: every output of `identify` and `show` and both
regressions are byte-identical.

- The core gains `residual` (moved from the harness, unchanged in
  arithmetic: the mask, the lags, *Align*, the gain fit; `Mask::UNDER` is
  session 70's frozen setting, *Align* and `over` 2) and
  `decode::Decoder::render` (a stretch of a file as a mix played it:
  FFmpeg's seek, then the harness's speed filters, to the profile's
  rate). The harness's residual maps its `Fix` onto the core's `Mask`
  and reproduces session 70's smoke report exactly.
- The CLI's `under` runs the step after the report is built, once per
  confident tracklist entry: the entry's strongest play's file (the
  best-aligned rip) is decoded from the library at each segment's line,
  aligned and masked; the residual is searched with the same index,
  ladder and rule. Dropped: finds of the entry's files; of files that
  share material with the file taken out by their stored peaks
  (possible tier or above, `search_peaks` against that file alone); of
  files confident within 90 s; and found plays that are one track with a
  confident play within 90 s (ADR 0017's tests: lining up or tagged
  alike).
- The printing: a confident find is a tracklist entry at its start,
  marked `(under N.)` with the louder track's entry number; finds are
  grouped into entries as plays are; possible finds stay out of the
  tracklist. The table marks finds `under <file> (play N)` in time
  order; CSV gains a row per find and the columns `play` and
  `under_play` only when the step ran; JSON holds `under` (each find with `under: {play, asset}`) and
  `search.under`, so `--save-dir` searches again when the setting
  changes. Cue sheet and timeline leave finds out.
- `--store-only`, no library, or an unreadable library: the step does
  not run, with one line on stderr.

In short: the option finds the quiet partner the measured residual
finds, prints it as its own tracklist entry under the louder one, and
adds nothing to the Stakka mix's output at either scale. It costs about
1-3 s per confident entry (experiment 0272); session 73 decides whether
that is acceptable by default.

### What changed

peaks are searched against the whole index the harness builds, on the
turntable ladder (±8%), once with every posting list and once with the
fullest 1% set aside as the first pass sets them aside (ADR 0014). Each

strongest first, with its relative speed and where the passage lies in
it; the query's own duplicate cluster is listed apart; for the 20
strongest other files the whole query is measured against that file
alone (the *reach*: a rip or version goes on matching beyond the
passage). `scripts/analysis/break_search.py` scores the reports
(tested). Pre-registered in `56a2ec0` (brief 74), the cases committed
in `46e441c` (`docs/experiments/data/0276-cases.json`) before any
scored run.

In short: the passage query finds the judged shared material and the
user's Special Branch partners (59 of 64 positives at NAS scale, 51 in
the first five), but ranks the expected file first only 25 times. A
famous break's passage lines up with hundreds of files at similar
strength (up to 403, 1% of the index), and the files above an expected
one are mostly other rips, other versions and other holders of the same
break, not chance. The filter hides most of a famous break's holders
(Special Branch: 124 other files with every list, 25 with it); searching
every list costs a median 5.5 thread seconds per query alone at NAS
scale. By the registered bar it is not yet worth a design note and an
ADR.
