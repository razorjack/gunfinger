# 0298: a set-aside threshold by posting count, after two prunes and under growth

**Question** (brief 88, registered in `df1ce0e`). Does a fixed number of postings per list, replacing ADR 0014's fullest 1%, stop a library change from moving detections of untouched files?
**Method.** Variant V: lists above T = 9,932 postings are set aside (T derived once from today's whole NAS index, the longest list today's cut keeps; 10,865 of 1,086,456 lists; `work/indexes/library-nas-dnb-peaks.set-aside.json`, a sidecar, so ADR 0011's file format is unchanged). Today's rule R: the fullest 1% of each index searched. The prunes of sessions 64 (4 files) and 75 (8) are re-created in memory by `--replay-index` from the saved index before session 64's prune less the pruned files (`--replay-leave-out`); less the 12 it has today's lists, less the 4 those before session 75's prune (digests). `regress`-as-run per arm (sweep 720 queries; Stakka and its leave-outs of 3 and 11; 2003 and Nocny scans), 16 workers. `work/s88/queue.sh`, `compare.sh`, `refits.py`; data `data/0298-set-aside-threshold.json`.

| | Today's rule R | V (T = 9,932) |
|---|--:|--:|
| Prune of session 64: sweep queries identical, pruned files' own candidates set aside (of 720) | 713 | 707 |
| Prune of session 75: the same | 695 | 720 |
| Whole-index lists crossing the cut, prune 64; prune 75 | 2; 2 | 3; 3 |
| Lists set aside: before prune 64; after it; after prune 75 | 10,865; 10,863; 10,865 | 10,871; 10,868; 10,865 |
| Confident answer changed, of any file, in any arm | 0 | 0 |
| Largest change of a confident detection's hits | +14 of 303 (Dominion, prune 75) | -1 of 1,178 (Clockwork, prune 64) |

Replay fidelity: R after both prunes equals `session-78-linux-nas` in all 720 queries and every scan; 713 and 695 are 0254's 707 + 6 and 0278's 689 + 6 pruned files' own changes. `off-262` and `off-nas` with the new options unset: `regress --strict` identical.
- **Scans (Stakka, leave-outs 3 and 11, 2003, Nocny; pruned files set aside).** Neither rule changes a confident file or a confident detection's hits. Confident detections refit, in all five scans: R 4 and 4 (speeds by up to 6.5e-6 points); V 4 and 11 (up to 3.5e-5 points); with 1-3 weak detections moved in some scans under each rule. V moved more confident fits on the scans than R, in the same small way.
- **Why V does not stop it.** T is fixed, but a list holding a pruned file's postings shortens and can cross it (9,933 to 9,926, 9,936 to 9,914, ...), by construction exactly the lists that changed length. V removes only the moves made by the cut itself moving. In the whole index those were few already (R: 2 lists per prune); in the sweep's index and leave-outs the moving cut was traced as well (0254, 0278). So V trades one cause for the other: 13 of 1,440 sweep comparisons changed under V against 32 under R (bar 2 holds), but 13 in the first prune against 7 under R.
- **At the same index (D, one-time).** Whole-index scans identical under V and R. The sweep (720) and leave-outs carry their own cuts under R, so V differs: 534 queries identical, 186 changed (138 refits, 48 weak); leave-out 11 one weak more; no confident answer changed; 210 of 1,517 confident detections moved, 11 by hits (at most 1.17%).
- **Growth (exact copies of drawn records, sweep only).** 5% (2,034 copies, 1,198.1M postings, T kept): V sets aside 11,757 lists, 1.082% (bar 1.5%). Against R at the same grown index 642 of 720 queries change (536 refits, 106 weak), no confident file; the largest confident change of hits is 36% on one weak detection (Siren Bass at -3%, 248 to 337 hits, both confident; 0.2 points of speed). 20% (8,138 copies, 1,369.1M postings): the rule re-derives T (11,904, 10,864 lists, 1.000%); against R at the same index 178 of 720 change (128 refits, 50 weak), no confident file. With T left at 9,932 at 20%: 1.350% set aside (14,668 lists), 684 of 720 change, no confident file. Copies add postings, no hashes, so real growth is not shown.
- **262 tracks** (T = 118, 6,029 of 605,333 lists; 12 drawn corpus files, none in any manifest or sweep panel, 4.5% of the library): of 720 sweep queries, identical after the prune R 229, V 191; no confident file changes under either. A prune of that share moves most fits under either rule.

**Conclusion.** All four registered conditions hold: same lists at today's index (test and `at-D` whole-index scans); 13 against 32 of 1,440 sweep comparisons changed over two prunes (half is 16), no confident change; the one-time difference from R changes no confident answer; 5% growth 1.082% set aside and 20% re-derived, no confident answer differs. But V does not make a prune move nothing: one prune of the two moved more sweep queries (13 against 7), the scans' confident fits moved more (15 against 8), and lists crossing T by their own length stay; growth moves most queries' fits under either rule. The gain is smaller than the idea promised. ADR 0014's rule stays the default. An ADR would need the threshold in the saved index's header and a growth rule; decisions queue item 11.
